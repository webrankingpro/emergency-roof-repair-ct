Choose between a leak and storm request

Use the leak repair page when water is coming through a ceiling, a wall has become damp, or a stain changes after rainfall. It focuses on tracing the visible water path and protecting the room until a contractor can assess the roof. If the issue began after strong winds, use the storm and wind page to describe missing shingle material, disturbed flashing or impact from a branch.

Keep people away from immediate hazards

A ceiling that bulges, cracks or drops material should not be approached. Leave the room and prevent others from entering. If water is near a light, outlet or exposed wire, stay clear and seek appropriate help for the electrical concern. Move belongings only when the route is safe, and use a stable container for a drip only when the ceiling is sound.

Regional weather and roof condition

Connecticut weather can bring strong coastal storms, heavy snow and repeated freezing and thawing. Ice dams may affect water movement near roof edges, while gusts can stress exposed roof details. These regional conditions help frame a timeline but do not identify damage at a specific address.

Questions about New Britain roof requests

Do I need to know the roof material before choosing a page?

No. Start with the symptom you can observe. Explain whether water is entering or whether wind has visibly disturbed the roof. A contractor may ask about the roof material during the conversation.

What if water appears near a ceiling vent?

Stay away if there is moisture near electrical equipment. Photograph the area from a safe place and note whether water appears during rainfall. The roof opening may not be directly above the vent, so do not assume the vent itself is the cause.

Can I book a repair through this town hub?

The hub provides information and a way to call. A participating contractor may be introduced, and that contractor handles any inspection, schedule, pricing and agreement directly with the homeowner.
