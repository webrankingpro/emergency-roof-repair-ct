Independent contractors

When you call or submit a request, you may be connected with a participating local roofing contractor. Each contractor is an independent business responsible for its own inspection, services, pricing, licensing and insurance.

A call or request is not an inspection, quote, acceptance of a job or promise that a contractor will perform work. Contractors are separate businesses. They are responsible for explaining their services, evaluating the property, setting their prices and agreeing on any work directly with the homeowner.

Website information and safety

The site provides general information only. Roof conditions vary by building, material, weather and access. Content is not a substitute for an on-site assessment or professional advice about a specific property. Do not climb onto a wet, icy or wind-damaged roof. Stay away from wet electrical fixtures and unstable ceilings. Contact the appropriate emergency service for an immediate threat to people, utilities or the building.

Calls, texts and consent

When you submit a request through the form on this website, you consent to be contacted as described in the consent wording shown with the form. That wording reads:

By clicking Request emergency help, I give my express consent to be contacted by Emergency Roof Repair CT and participating roofing contractors by phone call and text message, including by automated dialing technology or prerecorded or artificial voice, at the number I entered, about my roofing request. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of texts. See our Privacy Policy and Terms.
