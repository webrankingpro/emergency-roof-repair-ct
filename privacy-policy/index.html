Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Emergency Roof Repair CT may handle information connected with calls, website visits and requests for roof repair help.

Information received through calls and forms

A call or form may provide a name, mobile number, property ZIP code, selected roof issue and other details a caller chooses to share. The website may also receive basic technical information such as browser, device, pages visited and referring website. Do not send payment details or sensitive personal information through this site.

Call routing, tracking and recording

Calls may be routed or tracked to understand which page or campaign led to a contact and to connect callers with participating roofing contractors. Calls may be monitored or recorded where the call platform or a participating contractor uses those features.

How information may be used

Information may be used to respond to a request, route a call, check whether a participating contractor may serve a location, maintain website operations, prevent misuse and keep service records. Information may be handled by providers supporting hosting, forms, analytics or call routing.

Sharing with roofing contractors

When a caller asks for roof repair help, relevant details may be shared with a participating roofing contractor so it can decide whether to respond. The contractor is a separate business and may handle information under its own privacy notice. Service providers may also process information to support the website or the process of connecting callers with contractors.

Retention, security and choices

Information may be kept for as long as needed for service operations, records, dispute handling or legal obligations. No online system can be described as completely secure. Access should be limited to those who need the information to operate the service. A visitor may choose not to use the form and may call instead. Privacy questions can be raised by calling the number shown on this site.

Children and updates

This service is intended for adults seeking help with a property roof issue and is not designed to collect information from children. This policy was last updated in November 2026 and may be updated from time to time; the current version is always shown on this page.
