What this service does

The site provides information about roof leaks, storm and wind damage, flat roof problems and temporary roof protection. A caller can share a property ZIP code and describe the issue. When a suitable contractor is available, the caller may be connected with a participating roofing contractor who serves the area.

What this service does not do

Emergency Roof Repair CT connects callers with independent roofing contractors. We do not set a contractor's pricing or decide which work a property needs. The contractor is a separate business and is solely responsible for its own inspection, services, pricing, licensing and insurance.

A call or request is not a quote, inspection or confirmation that a contractor has accepted the job. Ask the contractor directly about its assessment, scope and terms before agreeing to work. The information on this website is general and does not replace an on-site assessment of a particular roof.

Serving Connecticut

The site is focused on Connecticut. Town pages describe the areas we serve, and coverage depends on the property's ZIP code and a participating contractor's service area. A town listing does not claim a physical office or local crew.

About the contractors

When you call or submit a request, you may be connected with a participating local roofing contractor. Each contractor is an independent business responsible for its own inspection, services, pricing, licensing and insurance.
