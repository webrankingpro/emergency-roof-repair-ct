Check the property ZIP code

The county and ZIP details displayed on the town pages are for general location reference. Share the property's actual ZIP code when you call so coverage can be confirmed. A town name on this website is not proof that a contractor has an office in that location.

What to have ready

Describe whether water is entering the home, the roof has visible storm damage, or a low-slope roof or temporary covering is involved. Move valuables only when safe, avoid wet electrical fixtures and do not climb onto a wet or icy roof. Take photographs from a safe place and note when the issue began.
